1. What is Data?

Data is information. Your name, your age, your phone number, your favourite food - all of that is data.

Example: Mary, 20, Ruiru, loves Chapati = 4 pieces of data.

2. Types of Data

1. Numbers: Age, Height, Price - e.g. 20, 165cm, 100 Ksh

2. Words: Name, Town, Colour - e.g. Mary, Ruiru, Blue

3. Yes / No: Do you like data? Yes / No

3. Why Data Matters

Data helps us make better decisions. Shops use data to know what to sell. Doctors use data to help patients. You can use data to grow your business.

4. Your First Lesson

Think of 3 data about YOU. Write them down. You just created your first dataset!
